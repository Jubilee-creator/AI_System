# /client-audit

Purpose:
Analyze a website like a high-level agency and identify what is killing conversions, clarity, and trust.

Use when:
Samuel wants to review a landing page, product page, or client site.

---

## AUDIT PROCESS

1. First Impression (0–3 seconds)
- Is it clear what this is?
- Who is it for?
- What action should I take?

If not clear → critical issue.

---

2. Offer Clarity
- What is being sold?
- Is the value obvious?
- Is the messaging simple and direct?

---

3. Conversion Flow
- Does the page guide the user logically?
- Is there a clear CTA?
- Are there too many distractions?

---

4. Design Quality
- Does it look premium or cheap?
- Is spacing consistent?
- Is typography clean?
- Does it feel like a real brand?

---

5. Trust Signals
- Testimonials
- Reviews
- Social proof
- Guarantees

Missing trust = lower conversions.

---

6. Mobile Experience
- Is it readable on mobile?
- Are buttons easy to click?
- Is layout broken or cramped?

---

7. Speed + Performance
- Slow load = lost users
- Heavy UI = worse conversions

---

## IDENTIFY PROBLEMS

List:
- what feels confusing
- what feels cheap
- what breaks trust
- what kills conversion

---

## OUTPUT STRUCTURE

1. OVERALL VERDICT
- premium / average / weak

2. TOP 10 PROBLEMS
- ranked by impact

3. QUICK WINS (fix immediately)
- high impact, low effort

4. HIGH IMPACT FIXES
- biggest improvements to conversion

5. WHAT TO IGNORE
- things that don’t matter

6. PRIORITY PLAN
- what to fix first → last

---

## QUALITY CHECK

Before finishing:
- Would this convert in real life?
- Would a serious brand ship this?
- Is the feedback actionable or generic?

Reject generic advice.
